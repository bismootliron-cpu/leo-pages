Questions

Before you subscribe

What happens during the 3 free days?

You get the same thing members get — indicator access and the live signal channel. Nothing is held back for the trial, because the point is to let you judge the real feed.

What if I want to cancel after paying?

Billing stops and access ends the moment you cancel — there's no separate process to chase.

Do I need a paid TradingView plan?

A free TradingView account is enough to receive and view the indicator. Paid plans matter if you want more indicators per chart or server-side alerts — that's a TradingView limit, not ours.

How fast do I get access?

The Telegram invite is issued automatically. Indicator access is added to your TradingView username shortly after — it's a manual grant on our side, so allow a little time rather than expecting it the same second.

What markets and timeframes does it cover?

The indicator applies to whatever you put it on. The signal channel currently focuses on crypto perpetuals across intraday timeframes; the channel history shows exactly what has been covered.

Does it repaint?

No. Once a signal prints it stays printed, and the Telegram message is sent at that moment — which is also why the channel history can be used as a record.

Why is there no win rate on this page?

Because publishing one right now would mean inventing it. The live sample isn't long enough to state a rate we'd stand behind. When there is enough data, it will be published with the method attached.

Can I automate it?

Not through us. There's no broker connection and no auto-execution — every trade is placed by you.

Is this financial advice?

No. Signals are informational. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell, and every decision — including whether to act on a signal at all — is yours.
